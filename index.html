Welcome to De Portal Schools

A Culture of Distinction, Diligence and Integrity

At De Portal Schools, we believe every child is endowed with boundless potential waiting to be ignited. Located in the serene town of Ogijo, Ogun State, our institution has stood as an oasis of rigorous academics, Christian moral values, and wholesome character formation.

From our Early Years foundation where curiosity is cultivated, through Junior Secondary where BECE candidates achieve distinctions, to our Senior Secondary College where students excel in Science, Arts, and Commercial tracks for WAEC and JAMB — our dedicated educators nurture every learner with individualized care.
